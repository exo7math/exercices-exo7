\exercice{753, ridde, 1999/11/01}

\enonce
Montrer que \( \dfrac{\pi}4 = 4\arctan (\dfrac 15) - \arctan (\dfrac 1{239}) \) (on montrera que \( 0 \le \arctan (\dfrac 15) \le \dfrac{\pi}8 \) et \( 0 \le \arctan (\dfrac 1{239}) \le \dfrac{\pi}2 \)).
\finenonce

\noindication

\nocorrection

\finexercice
