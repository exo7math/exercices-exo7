\exercice{5007, quercia, 2010/03/17}

\enonce[Branches infinies]
Déterminer les branches infinies pour les courbes paramétrées suivantes :
\begin{enumerate}
  \item\( x = 4t^5-4t^3+t \), \( y = \dfrac{t}{3t^4+1} \)

  \item \( x = 2\cos^2t+\ln|\sin t| \), \( y = \sin 2t \)

  \item\( x = \sqrt{\dfrac{t^2-2}{t^4-1}} \), \( y = tx \)

  \item \( x = \dfrac{t^3-t}{2t-1} \), \( y = tx \)

  \item\( x = \dfrac1t+\dfrac1{t+1} \), \( y = \dfrac1t+\dfrac1{(t+1)^2} \)

  \item \( x = \sin \dfrac t2 \), \( y = \tan t \)

  \item\( x = \dfrac1t+\dfrac1{t+1} \), \( y = \dfrac1t-\dfrac1{t+1} \)

  \item\( x = \dfrac{3t}{1+t^3} \), \( y = tx \)

  \item\( x = \dfrac{te^t}{t+1} \), \( y = \dfrac{e^t}{t+1} \)

  \item\( x = 2t^3+3t^2 \), \( y = 3t^2+6t \)

  \item\( x = t^3-3t \), \( y = t^3-t^2-t+1 \)

  \item\( x = \dfrac t{t^2-1} \), \( y = \dfrac{t^2}{t-1} \)
\end{enumerate}
\finenonce
