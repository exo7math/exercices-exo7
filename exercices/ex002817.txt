\exercice{2817, burnol, 2009/12/15}

\enonce
En utilisant \( I_{m+1}\le J_m\le I_m \), obtenir:
\[
  \frac{2m+1}{2m+2}
  \frac{(1\cdot3\cdot\cdots\cdot(2m-1))(3\cdot5\cdot\cdots\cdot(2m+1))}{(2\cdot4\cdot\cdots\cdot(2m))^2}\le
  \frac2\pi \le
  \frac{(1.3.\cdots.(2m-1))(3.5.\cdots.(2m+1))}{(2.4.\cdots.(2m))^2}
\]
En déduire la formule de Wallis:
\[
  \frac2\pi = \lim_{m\to\infty}
  \frac{1\cdot3}{2\cdot2}\frac{3\cdot5}{4\cdot4}\cdots\frac{(2m-1)\cdot(2m+1)}{(2m)\cdot(2m)}
  = \prod_{m = 1}^\infty \left( 1 - \frac1{4m^2}\right)
\]
\finenonce

\noindication

\nocorrection

\finexercice
