\exercice{4205, quercia, 2010/03/11}

\enonce[\( x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = -y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \)]
Résoudre sur \( {\Rr^2} \setminus \{(0,0)\} \) : \( x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = -y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \), en posant \( \begin{cases}x = \rho\cos\theta \\ y = \rho\sin\theta. \end{cases} \)
\finenonce

\noindication

\correction
\( f(x,y) = g(\theta) \).
\fincorrection

\finexercice
