\exercice{1907, ridde, 1999/11/01}

\enonce
Calculer \( I_1 = \iint\limits_D (x + y)e^{-x}e^{-y}dxdy \) où \( D = \left\{ (x, y)\in \Rr^2 \mid x, y\ge 0, x + y \le 1\right\} \).

Calculer \( I_2 = \iint\limits_D (x^2 + y^2)dxdy \) où \( D = \left\{ (x, y)\in \Rr^2 \mid x^2 + y^2<x, x^2 + y^2>y\right\} \).

Calculer \( I_3 = \iint\limits_D \frac{xy}{1 + x^2 + y^2}dxdy \) où \( D = \left\{ (x, y)\in [0, 1]^2 \mid x^2 + y^2 \ge 1\right\} \).

Calculer \( I_4 = \iint\limits_D \frac{1}{y\cos(x) + 1}dxdy \) où \( D = [0, \frac{\pi}{2}]\times [0, \frac{1}{2}] \).

Calculer \( I_5 = \iiint\limits_D zdxdydz \) où \( D = \left\{ (x, y, z)\in (\Rr^{+})^3 \mid y^2 + z \le 1, x^2 + z \le 1\right\} \).

Calculer \( I_6 = \iint\limits_D xydxdy \) où \( D = \left\{ (x, y)\in \Rr^2 \mid x, y>0, \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2}
\le 1\right\} \) avec \( a, b>0 \).
\finenonce

\noindication

\nocorrection

\finexercice
