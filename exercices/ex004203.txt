\exercice{4203, quercia, 2010/03/11}

\enonce[\( x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \)]
Résoudre sur \( (\Rr^{+*})^2 \) : \( x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \), en posant \( \begin{cases} u = xy \\ v = \frac xy. \end{cases} \)
\finenonce

\noindication

\correction
\( f(x,y) = g(xy) \).
\fincorrection

\finexercice
