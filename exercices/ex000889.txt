\exercice{889, legall, 1998/09/01}

\enonce
Parmi les ensembles suivants, reconna\^{\i}tre ceux qui sont des sous-espaces vectoriels :\\
\( \begin{array}{ll}
  E_1 = \{(x,y,z)\in \Rr^3/ x+y = 0\};        & E_1' = \{(x,y,z)\in \Rr^3/xy = 0\}.                 \\
  E_2 = \{(x,y,z,t)\in \Rr^4/ x = 0, y = z\}; & E_2' = \{(x,y,z)\in \Rr^3/x = 1\}.                  \\
  E_3 = \{(x,y)\in \Rr^2/ x^2+xy\ge 0\};      & E_3' = \{(x,y)\in \Rr^2/x^2+xy+y^2\ge 0\}.          \\
  E_4 = \{f\in \Rr^\Rr/ f(1) = 0\};           & E_4' = \{f\in \Rr^\Rr/ f(0) = 1\};                  \\
                                              & E_4'' = \{f\in \Rr^\Rr/ f \mbox{ est croissante}\}.
\end{array} \)
\finenonce

\noindication

\nocorrection

\finexercice
